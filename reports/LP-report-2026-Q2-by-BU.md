## Executive summary

The monthly source reports contain **52 publication records** in Q2 2026, representing **51 unique URLs**. The records produce **59 BU assignments** because cross-platform content can count toward more than one BU. One learning path, *Deploy containerized workloads to Arm-based Linux targets with Topo*, appears in both May and June and is retained on both publication dates so that the report reconciles to the monthly sources.
